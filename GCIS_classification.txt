In our HomeSense program, We have classified the readings as High or Critical as follows, 

If the device's energy consumption is within the normal range, then the devices are in normal condition.
Our team has decided that if a device's energy consumption is up to 50% higher than the maximum value in the normal range for the respective device, then the reading is determined to be “High”. And if the device's energy consumption is 50% higher than the maximum value in the normal range, the reading is “Critical”.
